### 💰 FINOVA
**Personal Finance Manager**

A personal finance application that combines transaction tracking, budgeting and financial analytics in one interface.

### 📧 ReachInbox Email Scheduler
**Email Campaign Scheduling Platform**

A full-stack application for scheduling email campaigns and tracking background jobs through a dashboard and REST API.

**Key features**
- Sender management and SMTP configuration.
- Delayed email jobs with BullMQ and Redis.
- Retry handling designed to prevent duplicate email sends.
- Recovery of pending jobs after a server restart.
- Configurable concurrency and per-sender rate limits.
- Slack OAuth notifications and Elasticsearch-powered email search.

**Technology stack**

`TypeScript` `React` `Express` `BullMQ` `Redis` `MySQL` `Elasticsearch`
